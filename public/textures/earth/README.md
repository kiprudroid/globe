## Resolution

All textures are 2K equirectangular (2048×1024 px), providing a good balance between visual quality and bundle size.

## Attribution

Textures in this pack are based on NASA elevation and imagery data. Earth textures are a result of merging and adjusting large amounts of geo-data, space photos, and images from NASA's Blue Marble.
